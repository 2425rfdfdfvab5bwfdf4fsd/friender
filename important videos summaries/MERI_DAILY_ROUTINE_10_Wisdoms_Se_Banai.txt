================================================================================
  ⏰ SUBAH KA WAQT — "FOUNDATION BLOCK"
  (Fajr ke baad ~ 5:30 AM se 8:00 AM)
================================================================================

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5:30 AM — UTHNA: PHONE NAHI, PEHLE 15 MINUTE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Arthur Brooks (Boredom) + Self-Mastery Coach

* Uthne ke fauran baad phone NAHI uthana — yeh 15 minute ka "boredom" kaafi
  qeemti hai; Default Mode Network on hota hai aur tumhara dimagh apne asli
  sawaalon ki taraf jaata hai
* Khidki ke paas jaao, pani piyo, thodi chhalni roshni mein baitho
* Sirf yeh 15 minute — koi notification, koi news, koi scroll nahi
* WHY: Subah ka pehla input tumhare poore din ki "magnetic north" set karta hai;
  agar woh news ya social media ho, toh din bhar negativity ka compass

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5:45 AM — MEDITATION / TM — 20 MINUTE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: David Lynch (Transcendental Meditation) + Tal Ben-Shahar (SPIRE)

* Khamoshi mein baitho — aankhen band
* Agar TM seekhi hai: Mantra use karo (effortless, forced nahi)
* Agar TM nahi seekhi: Sirf apni saans par dhyan do — andar, bahar; koi force
  nahi, sirf observe karo
* 20 minute kafi hain — timer lagao aur baith jaao
* WHY (David Lynch): "Har gehra level of mind mein zyada happiness hai — is
  liye mind naturally gehra jaata hai, jaise paani dhalta hai"; negativity
  (anger, anxiety, depression) bina koshish ke "lift away" hoti hai
* WHY (SPIRE): Physical wellbeing ke liye recovery essential hai — aur
  meditation deepest recovery hai

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6:05 AM — TEEN SUBAH KE SAWAAL (STOIC MIRROR CHECK)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Steve Jobs (Stanford) + Marcus Aurelius (Stoicism) + Guy Ritchie (Identity)

Aaina dekho ya aankhein band karke teen sawaal poochho:

SAWAAL 1 (Steve Jobs):
"Agar aaj meri zindagi ka aakhri din hota — toh kya main wahi karta jo main
aaj karne waala hoon?"
→ Agar jawab "Nahi" hai kaafi dino se — kuch badalna hai

SAWAAL 2 (Marcus Aurelius):
"Aaj main apne poore potential ke mutabiq jiuunga — woh version jis par
main sachchi mein believe karta hoon?"
→ Yeh tumhara daily compass hai — duty, virtue, character

SAWAAL 3 (Guy Ritchie):
"Aaj main jo bhi karunga — kya woh meri apni marzi se hoga, ya doosron ko
impress karne ke liye?"
→ Agar koi kaam forced lagta hai toh: "Main isko consciously own karta hoon"

* WHY: Yeh teen sawaal milke tumhara inner compass reset karte hain subah ko;
  din bhar baahri noise se pehle andar ki awaaz sun lete ho

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6:10 AM — PURPOSE REMINDER (30 SECOND)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Tal Ben-Shahar (SPIRE — Spiritual) + Ethan Hawke (Creativity)

Ek chhoti note ya mental reminder:
"Main aaj yeh sab kyun kar raha hoon?"
→ Apna "WHY" yaad karo — family, passion, impact, growth — jo bhi tumhara hai
→ Agar passion-based kaam chhoda hua hai: "Aaj main us cheez mein waqt dunga
   jo mujhe genuinely alive feel karwati hai"

* WHY: SPIRE kehti hai — "Jo insaan subah purpose ke saath utha, woh mushkilaat
  ko zyada aasani se paar karta hai"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6:15 AM — JISM KI ZARURAT: NEEND, EXERCISE, PAANI
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Tal Ben-Shahar (SPIRE — Physical) + Stephen Duneier (Marginal Adjustments)

* Paani piyo (neend mein body dehydrate hoti hai)
* Exercise — gym ya walk — 30-45 minute
  → Walk hai toh: Phone band karo, sirf khayal aao (Arthur Brooks)
  → Ya walk mein: Ek educational podcast / audiobook (dead time use karna —
    Stephen Duneier ki German waali soch; 360 ghante/saal milte hain sirf
    walk se)
* WHY: "Masla stress nahi — masla recovery ka na hona hai" (Tal Ben-Shahar);
  exercise physical recovery hai; aur yeh ek "marginal adjustment" hai jo
  compound karta hai

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7:30 AM — POSITIVE INPUT: 20-30 MINUTE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Self-Mastery Coach + Ethan Hawke (Creativity)

* Ek positive cheez consume karo: Kitaab, podcast, motivational interview,
  documentary — jo bhi tumhe genuinely inspire kare
* RULE: Jo kitaab CHAHTE ho padho — jo "padhni chahiye" nahi (Ethan Hawke)
* NEWS BILKUL NAHI — news dekhna aur positive rehna = binary contradiction
  (Self-Mastery Coach: "It's like saying I want to be healthy and I smoke")
* WHY: Subah ka input tumhara "compass needle" set karta hai; galat input =
  poora din negative magnetic north par; sahi input = compass positive rehi

  ⏰ DIN KA KAAM — "EXECUTION BLOCK"
  (8:00 AM se 6:00 PM)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8:00 AM — DIN KA PEHLA BADA KAAM (MOST IMPORTANT TASK)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Stephen Duneier (Marginal Adjustments) + Marcus Aurelius (Stoicism)

* Apna sabse important kaam — woh jo tumhe "stuck" feel kara raha ho — pehle
  karo jab energy highest hai
* DUNEIER METHOD: Ise 5-10 minute ke chunks mein todo:
  → 5-10 min kaam karo
  → 2-3 min break
  → 5-10 min kaam karo (wapas)
  → Continuous 2 ghante concentrate karne ki koshish mat karo
* Bade goal ko chhotay qadam mein dekhna:
  "Mujhe sirf YEH ek paragraph likhna hai" — poori book nahi
* MARCUS ki soch: Complaint nahi — sirf karo; "Agar yeh endure ho sakta hai,
  toh karo. Agar nahi, toh complaint se bhi kuch nahi banega"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10:00 AM — COSMIC PAUSE (5 MINUTE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Alex Hormozi (Resilience & Mood)

Jab koi cheez frustrating lage ya stuck feel ho:

* "Main ek planet par hoon jo ek sun ke gird ghoom raha hai, ek galaxy mein,
  ek universe mein jo light se bhi tez expand ho rahi hai"
* Is scale par — yeh email, yeh meeting, yeh mistake — kitni chhoti cheez hai
* Ek lambi saans lo aur wapas kaam par

* WHY (Hormozi): "Cosmic Relevance Tool — yeh thought almost automatically bura
  asar kam kar deta hai"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THROUGHOUT THE DAY — COMPLAINT FILTER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Alex Hormozi (Resilience)

Jab bhi complain karne ka mann kare, filter lagao:

LEVEL 1: Circumstances ko blame? → Control se bahar — chodo
LEVEL 2: Logon ko blame?         → Unhe change nahi kar sakte — chodo
LEVEL 3: Khud ko blame?          → Yahi actionable hai — yahan kaam karo

* RULE: Complain karoge toh dono lose karoge — na masla solve hoga, na mood
  achha rahega; "Jitna zyada complain, utna inaccurate reality ka model"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
COMMUTE / DEAD TIME — DEAD TIME MANAGEMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Stephen Duneier (Marginal Adjustments)

* Roz kitna "dead time" hai? (Commute, wait, errands, cooking, cleaning)
* Estimate: Average 1-1.5 ghante = 360-540 ghante/saal
* Is waqt mein: Educational podcast, audiobook, language learning app
* Stephen Duneier ne walk mein German CDs se 10 mahine mein German seekhi —
  sirf dead time use karke
* OPTIONS HATAO: Jo cheez distract kare usse environment se nikaalo

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1:00 PM — LUNCH BREAK — PHONE FREE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Arthur Brooks (Boredom) + SPIRE (Relational)

* Khane ke waqt phone BILKUL nahi
* Agar akele: Boredom ko allow karo — 15-20 minute ki genuine boredom; Default
  Mode Network ko kaam karne do
* Agar kisi ke saath: Phone band — quality time; "Number one predictor of
  happiness = quality time with people we care about" (Tal Ben-Shahar)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2:00 PM — MIDDAY ENERGY DIP — SAHI HANDLE KARO
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: SPIRE (Physical) + Tal Ben-Shahar

* Midday energy drop normal hai — force nahi karo
* Options:
  → 15-20 min rest / nap (body ki zarurat)
  → Walk (bina phone ke)
  → Koi creative / lighter kaam
* NAHI karna: Social media scroll, news, junk content — yeh energy aur kam
  karega

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2:30 PM — POSITIVE MOMENT ABSORB KARO (HEAL METHOD)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Alex Hormozi (HEAL — Rick Hansen Method)

Din mein koi bhi achha moment aaye — chota ya bada:
H — Have it: Consciously notice karo — "Yeh acha lag raha hai"
E — Enrich it: 20-30 second is feeling mein raho — sirf mind mein nahi, body
               mein feel karo (below the neck)
A — Absorb it: Imagine karo woh feeling andar sink ho rahi hai
L — Link (optional): Ise kisi bure experience se compare karo — bura chhota
                      lagega, acha bada

* WHY: Negativity bias ka counter — positives ko consciously register karna
  hota hai warna woh pass ho jaate hain

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4:00 PM — CREATIVE / PASSION TIME — 30-45 MINUTE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Ethan Hawke (Creativity) + Guy Ritchie (Identity) + Steve Jobs (Passion)

* Har din kuch waqt un cheezon ko do jo tumhe genuinely alive feel karaati hain
* Koi judgment nahi — woh hobby, writing, music, sketching, coding, craft —
  jo bhi tumhara hai
* RULE: "Ahmaq" lagna creativity ki qeemat hai — mat daro (Ethan Hawke)
* Khud se poochho: "Kya main yeh apni marzi se kar raha hoon?" — agar haan,
  toh yeh tumhara asli waqt hai (Guy Ritchie — ownership)
* Steve Jobs: Curiosity ko follow karo bina result sooche — dots baad mein
  connect honge

* WHY: Nani ki kahani (Ethan Hawke) — woh 5 pages jo costume banane par likhe
  gaye; jo cheez tumhein zinda rakhti hai wahi tumhari real story hai

* Sirf unke saath raho jinke dreams tumhare liye tumhare apne se zyada bade hain

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7:00 PM — NEWS / SOCIAL MEDIA BILKUL BAND
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Arthur Brooks + Self-Mastery Coach

* Raat 7 baje ke baad: News, breaking news, Twitter, negative social media —
  sab band (Arthur Brooks khud yahi karte hain)
* WHY: "You cannot watch the news and be positive — it's like I want to be

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7:30 PM — SHAAM KI MEDITATION / TM — 20 MINUTE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: David Lynch (TM — do baar daily)

* Doosra meditation session — subah wali hi technique
* WHY: Din bhar ki stress aur input ka "digest" hota hai
* Phir se: Effortless, forced nahi; timer lagao aur baith jaao
* David Lynch: "34 saalon se kar raha hoon — creativity, anger management,
  inner peace mein zamin-aasmaan ka fark hai"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8:00 PM — CURIOSITY TIME: DEEP READING / LEARNING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Ethan Hawke + SPIRE (Intellectual) + Arthur Brooks

* Woh kitaab jo CHAHTE ho — woh nahi jo "padhni chahiye" (Ethan Hawke)
* Ya kisi subject mein deep dive — nature, art, history, science — jo bhi
  genuinely curious kare
* Deep engagement = flow state = khushi ki peak condition (SPIRE)
* RULE: Agar bore ho raha ho — allow karo; Default Mode Network ko boredom se
  activate hone do; ideas wahan se aate hain (Arthur Brooks)
* Phone rakh do — sirf book ya notebook

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9:00 PM — MARGINAL IMPROVEMENT CHECK (10 MINUTE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: Stephen Duneier (Marginal Adjustments)

Roz shaam ek chhota review:
* "Aaj ka sabse bada bada kaam kya tha — kiya?"
* "Kal mera pehla qadam kya hoga?" (Sirf pehla qadam — poora plan nahi)
* "Meri dead time mein kya improvement ki?" (Commute, walk, etc.)
* "Kya koi ek marginal adjustment hai jo main kal try kar sakta hoon?"

* WHY (Djokovic principle): 49% se 55% decision success rate — yahi world #1
  bana. Sirf roz ek chhoti improvement, lifelong badaltay results

9:30 PM — GRATITUDE JOURNAL — 3 CHEEZEIN + WHY

Source: Tal Ben-Shahar (SPIRE — Emotional) + Alex Hormozi

1. Pehli cheez jo main thankful hoon: _________________ — WHY: _________________
2. Doosri cheez: _________________ — WHY: _________________

RULES:
* Superficial nahi — genuinely feel karo
* Roz alag cheezein dhundho — nahi toh boring ho jaayega
* WHY likhna zaroori hai — sirf cheez likhne se woh feeling nahi aati
* OPTIONAL: Hormozi method — "Koi buri cheez imagine karo jo nahi hui —
  phir yaad karo: yeh NAHI hua" — yeh instant gratitude hai

* WHY (Cicero / Tal Ben-Shahar): "Gratitude is the mother of all virtues;
  when we appreciate the good in our life, we have more of it"

Source: Marcus Aurelius (Meditations) + Steve Jobs

* WHY (Marcus): "When no one was looking — that's when Marcus was at his best";
  raat ko yeh check agle din ki foundation rakhta hai

================================================================================
  📅 HAFTEWAR ROUTINE — "WEEKLY RITUALS"
================================================================================

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HAR HAFTE (Koi bhi ek din fix karo):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ENVIRONMENT AUDIT (30 minute) — Source: Self-Mastery Coach
* Ghar: Kya koi distracting ya negative cheez remove ki ja sakti hai?
* Digital: Apps delete karo jo time waste karti hain; unfollow karo jo
  negative feel karaate hain
* Social: Kya mera waqt un logon ke saath hai jinke dreams mere liye mere
  apne se zyada bade hain? (Hormozi rule)
* Rule: "Love your family. Choose your friends."

BIG GOAL REVIEW (20 minute) — Source: Stephen Duneier
* Apna ek bada ambitious goal check karo
* Kya pehla qadam kiya?
* Agli hafte ka pehla qadam kya hoga?
* Dead time use ho rahi hai?
* Environment designed hai goal ke liye?

SPIRE WEEKLY CHECK — Source: Tal Ben-Shahar
Is hafte mein kaisi rahi har dimension?
S — Spiritual: Kya koi purpose feel kiya?
P — Physical: Recovery schedule follow hua?
I — Intellectual: Koi deep curiosity ya learning?
R — Relational: Quality time kisi close insaan ke saath?
E — Emotional: Gratitude journal roz hua? Painful emotions process kiye?
Sabse weak dimension par agli hafte zyada dhyan

NO-TECH DAY ya HALF-DAY — Source: Arthur Brooks
* Hafte mein ek baar kuch ghante — ya ek poora din — phones/screens se door
* Yeh "boredom" ka sabse bada booster hai
* Outdoors jaao, books padho, log milao, kuch banao
